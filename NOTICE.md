# yysort

Copyright (C) yyeditor contributors. GPL-3.0-or-later; repository COPYING and
COPYING.EXCEPTION apply. yysort reuses yyeditor's yy-encoding codecs, including
the same Japanese/EBCDIC tables and custom mapping mechanism.

DFSORT is an IBM product. yysort is an independent implementation of a documented
subset of its control-card interface. It does not contain IBM product source,
runtime binaries, JCL execution infrastructure, or a complete DFSORT implementation.

Distribution bundles dependency license/notice texts in `licenses/`, generated
from the Cargo.lock dependency graph by `tools/licenses.py`.
